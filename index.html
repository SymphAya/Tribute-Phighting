Meet The Factions Of Phighting!

Crossroads

Crossroads is neutral ground, Inphernals from all over can move into Crossroads for a safer life. Not only is it neutral ground, but it is also the safest place in the Inpherno to live. All the Phighters live in Crossroads. Venomshank lives here with his son, Sword, and Zuka lives in Crossroads with his son, Rocket.

Playground

Playground is possibly the most dangerous, or it comes in second place. Playground has anarchy, known for gangs and violen Not only are there gangs, but you can find Underground Boxing Rings that Coil and the NPC known as Punk are part of. The Phighters Coil, Skateboard, and Boombox are all from Playground.

Theives Den

Thieves Den is known for its Megafauna, making it dangerous with the wildlife. There are also clans in Theives Den, such as the Darkage Clan. The Phighters Vinestaff, Shuriken, Katana, and Slingshot are from Theives Den.

Lost Temple

Lost Temple is best known for the event called The Scorch. Ten years ago, Lost Temple was suddenly burned to the ground and eternal flames remain in the ruins. After the Scorch, Father Overseer appeared and promised a paradise. Now, the Redcliff Wranglers and Cult Of The True Eye are at war. The Cult Of The True Eye has overtaken the Redcliff Wranglers's Spawn, leading to their conflict. The Redcliff Wranglers have allied with Theives Den in hopes to reclaim their spawn. Due to this conflict and the danger of the eternal flames in the ruins, Lost Temple is also one of the most dangerous in the Inpherno. The Phighters Medkit and Scythe are from Lost Temple, with Katana originally spawning in Lost Temple before the Scorch.

Blackrock

Blackrock is known for its military and scientific focus. Blackrock is run by the Korblox Administration. Biografts are made in Blackrock and distributed across the Inpherno from there. Blackrock is also known to be unethical. The Phighters Subspace and Biograft are from Blackrock, with Medkit originally spawning in Blackrock until Subspace betrayed him. Biografts were invented by Subspace, so he is known as their creator and Biografts are powered by crystals found in Blackrock.
