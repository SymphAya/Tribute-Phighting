The Redcliff Wranglers

Grappling Hook

Grappling Hook is the leader of the Redcliff Wranglers, he makes an appearance in Death In The Family when Sword travels out to Lost Temple with Darkheart. Grappling Hook seems to be a beloved leader among the wranglers and it seems multiple of the wranglers owe him something. Grappling Hook is also known to be Scythe's ex, and can be seen together in some of Sodakettle's art.

Tarantula

Tarantula, also known as Tara, is possibly the friendliest of the Wranglers. She is very sociable, nervous around strangers but easily warms up to new friends. Though, this does make her overly trusting of other Inphernals despite the reminders from her groupmates to not blindly trust others. Tara is very playful, not really taking things too seriously because she prefers to keep the mood light. If someone lets her, Tara will happily ramble on without end. Tara is a good friend of TNT, also the only Inphernal allowed to call him nicknames. Tara is the tallest of the wranglers as well. Before the Scorch, Tara used to raise animals for a living. Due to her previous job, Tara is meant to look after the oziphrages. Tara's gear is a little pet tarantula she named Chipper. Sometimes Tara will take scraps of fabric and make little hats for Chipper.

TNT

TNT is the most reckless of the Wranglers, and he's kind of stand-offish. TNT is automatically suspicious of outsiders, but also eager to poke fun at them. TNT is very friendly and loud with people they know, such as other Wranglers. TNT is good friends with Tara, since they have equally silly natures and Tara is the only one TNT allows to call them nickanmes because they're very close. TNT speaks informally, unless he's being sycophantic.

Chuck

Chuck is the youngest of the Wranglers, but he's also the shortest. Chuck likes to act tough because of short stature, but he holds a large amount of respect for Beehive and Grappling Hook. Chuck is an amazing sharpshooter, able to easily snipe a target and he has been known to do this when other Wranglers get on his nerves. Even though Chuck acts all tough, he still quietly returns affection from his groupmates. It's also seen with his Oziphrage, whom he babies when nobody's looking.

Caltrop

Caltrop, also known as Cal, is one of the highly-skilled members of the Wranglers. Cal takes a great amount of pride in his gear, small caltrops that can fit in the palm of his hand and easily go through someone's shoe. Cal has great precision and can throw very far with his gear, while also taking every job seriously and following orders stringently. Despite how Cal acts with his work, Cal is very social and loves outings with large groups while not being afraid to make jobs at other Inphernals. Even though Cal is very social, he doesn't blindly trust outsiders. Cal also has a minor lisp, and always keeps his sleeves rolled up even when he's told to roll them down. Cal is Cowbell's parent, also part of the Wranglers, and he loves them deeply. Cal's oziphrage is named Teddy.

This has Taratula, TNT, Chuck, and Caltrop who you can read about above.

Beehive

Beehive, also known as Bee, is possibly the most irritable of the Wranglers. She shows a very clear distaste for outsiders and she shows frustration towards Sword's lack of knowledge with the Church of The True Eye. Bee also holds the opinion that the Church has a bunch of "outlaws craving violence," being less sympathetic than Grappling Hook. Despite being aloof, Bee does have genuine care and wishes to protect the Wranglers even if she doesn't let any kind of vulnerability slip through.

Cowbell

Not much is known about Cowbell other than they are Caltrop's child. It is unknown if they are a natural spawn or unnatural spawn or even what they do.

The Cult Of The True Eye

Father Overseer

The Father Overseer makes very short appearances in Death In The Family. So far in the comic, he has two appearances. We see Father Overseer in the beginning with Broker during the call with Medkit. His second appearance occurs when Grappling Hook tells Sword the story of the Scorch. Father Overseer appeared when the Scorch occured, claiming to spawn without a gear and a promised paradise. Naturally, only on his path of enlightenment. Father Overseer is disliked by many of the Redcliff Wranglers.

The Sword Fight On The Heights Deities

Each one of them appears in game only for Sword Events. Even so, not all of them are available. Very few of them have appeared in Death In The Family so far, but it's expected we'll see more in the upcoming campaign or in future pages.

Extra notes! All of the Deities in Phighting are siblings, and deities are able to have their own biological spawns without sacrificing their gears.

There isn't much lore for Icedagger at the moment, but we do know that Icedagger is related to the Swords as siblings by blood. Banhammer and Sword are Icedagger's nephews with Dom and Valk being his grand nephews!

Firebrand

Firebrand was once the most beloved among the Sword Deities, known as the softie among them. However, despite Firebrand not wishing to harm or distress Inphernals, 10 years prior to Phighting, Firebrand caused what is known as the Scorch. The Scorch is an event that caused the destruction of one of Lost Temple's major cities, and the flames in the ruins will not dwindle so long as he lives. Due to the Scorch, the Swords are now prohibited from interacting with the general population of Inphernals and caused distrust among the public towards the Swords. It is unknown why Firebrand caused the Scorch in Lost Temple at this time. We also know Firebrand is the biological grandfather of Dom and Valk, also known as Flipside. Firebrand became their guardian when they were young, being fiercely protective of them. Firebrand acts more like a stand-in for their parents rather than only a grandparent. Ban Hammer and Sword are Firebrand's nephews, and Firebrand is close with Windforce.

Below is art from Phighting's official artists of Firebrand.

Venomshank

Between the deities of the Inpherno, Venomshank spends the most time in Crossroads, where many Inphernals live. Venomshank's plague mask can be locked, specifically so he doesn't accidentally bite others when he goes feral. When Venomshank is in his feral state, he's not fully conscious of his actions and behavior, only having vague memories hours after the feral state reaches an end. Venomshank's bite is able to turn others into zombies, but he doesn't quite have full control over it. Venomshank is Sword's mentor and guardian, with Banhammer being his nephew and Dom and Valk being his grand nephews. Venomshank is close with Darkheart, and he dislikes Illumina.

Below is art made by Phighting's official artists of Venomshank.

Windforce

Windforce is the most destructive of all the deities, only Firebrand really keeps her in check. Windforce doesn't care for fame and worship, simply doing as she pleases. Despite this, she chooses to be a good parent towards Banhammer, her son. Windforce is close with Firebrand, with Dom and Valk being her grand nephews and Sword being her nephew.

Below is art made by Phighting's official artists of Venomshank.

Ghostwalker

Ghostwalker doesn't have much, he is unable to feel regular emotions. Ghostwalker also doesn't exactly have a mouth, and no one knows how he speaks. Ghostwalker is the creator of the Watchers, such as Ghostdeeri and Doomsekkar. Banhammer and Sword are Ghostwalker's nephews, with Dom and Valk being his grand nephews. Ghostwalker also works with Illumina.

Below is art made by Phighting's official artists of Ghostwalker.

Darkheart

Darkheart is a very mischievous person, while also being a mysterious person who likes fishing. Darkheart also can be seen as someone who doesn't have a concept of boundaries, due to the events of Death In The Family. Darkheart also refers to himself plurally because he considers his gear to be a separate entity from himself. Darkheart is close with Venomshank, and previous worked with Zuka. Zuka saw Darkheart as a friend, but the feeling wasn't mutual. Darkheart saw Zuka as a plaything. Darkheart and Illumina dislike each other as well. Banhammer and Sword are Darkheart's nephews, with Dom and Valk being his Grand Nephews.

Below is art made by Sodakettle of Darkheart.

Illumina

Illumina may be incredibly powerful among the deities, he instead prefers to verbally break down whatever's facing him and scare them off without lifting a finger. Illumina finds it amusing, which is why he does it this way instead. Illumina currently has one wing, and it's unknown how he lost his second wing. Illumina is disliked by Venomshank, and he's neutral with Zuka despite Zuka despising him. Illumina also dislikes Darkheart, and works with Ghostwalker. Banhammer and Sword are Illumina's nephews, with Dom and Valk being his grand nephews.
