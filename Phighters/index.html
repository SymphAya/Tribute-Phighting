Meet The Phighters!!

Melee Phighters

Sword

Sword is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Katana

Katana is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Katana spawned in Lost Temple, spawning unnaturally and growing up with his two caretakers and sibling. Later on, once the scorch occured Katana went with Father Overseer and joined the Church Of The True eye. While Katana was at the church with his sibling, they were betrayed and kicked out. Katana left to Theives Den, later joining the Darkage clan and having the goal to erase the world of the corrupted.

Banhammer

Banhammer is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Banhammer is a demideity, he is a child of Windforce. Growing up, Banhammer was spoiled by his mother. He is also the warden of the Banlands, known as a tyrant there.

Skateboard

Skateboard is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Skateboard is from Playground, part of the trio with Coil and Boombox.

Biograft

Biograft is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Biografts are made in Blackrock, and they run the majority of Blackrock including the military. Biografts were invented by Subspace T. Mine, so Biografts refer to Subspace as Creator in-game. There are multiple models, such as Zetagrafts and Betagrafts. The ones in game are simply Biografts.

Ranged Phighters

Shuriken

Shuriken is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Shuriken is part of the Phighters from Theives Den, and Shuriken was part of the Darkage Clan with Vinestaff and Slingshot. They later left for Crossroads, and now Shuriken works at Slingshot's cafe known as Theives Rest. Shuriken also stays up late, acting as a vigilante known as the Silver Shadow. Shuriken is also Vinestaff's sibling.

Scythe

Scythe is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Scythe is part of the Church Of The True Eye alongside Medkit and Broker. Originally, Scythe was part of the Redcliff Wranglers, losing her arm in the Scorch. Scythe left to the Church Of The True Eye, which led to her parting ways with Grappling Hook, her romantic partner. Now, Scythe and Grappling Hook are exes. Scythe is higher in the ranks of the cult, she is above Medkit in ranking, so she is his boss.

Hyperlaser

Hyperlaser is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Hyperlaser comes from Blackrock, he is a gun for hire. Hyperlaser wears a helmet because he is incredibly nearsighted after an accident that left him burned across his body and with four stubs where his horns originally were. Subspace T. Mine created Hyperlaser's helmet, allowing Hyperlaser to continue as a gun for hire without the worry of being incredibly nearsighted. Hyperlaser also loves cats and even has one adopted that he named Princess. Hyperlaser spoils her.

Slingshot

Slingshot is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Slingshot is the owner of the cafe known as Theives Rest. Slingshot runs it with the help of Vinestaff and Shuriken, and he originally came from the Darkage Clan. Slingshot greatly values Shuriken and Vinestaff, even if Shuriken being late annoys him.

Rocket

Rocket is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Rocket spawned in Playground, and before he lived with Zuka, Rocket ended up blowing himself up with his gear, causing the loss of his arm and his leg. After the incident, Zuka found him and took him in, now living in Crossroads with Zuka. Rocket later met Sword and Medkit in Crossroads, and Medkit made Rocket's prosthetics that he uses today. Rocket makes an appearance in Death In The Family.

Support Phighters

Coil

Coil is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Coil spawned in Playground, part of the Playground trio with Boombox and Skateboard. Coil was raised by Wolf, a character we've not quite fully seen in the art made by Sodakettle. Coil was often called a violent spawn, and his full name is Gravity Coil. Coil can use the other coil gears because he learned how to use crystals from Blackrock to get the Speed and Regen Coils. Coil is also known as the Crystal Criminal by Blackrock, ad Coil is part of an underground boxing ring where he meets Punk.

Subspace

Subspace is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Subspace spawned in Blackrock, and he is often praised for his great brain. Subspace is the creator of the Biografts and Hyperlaser's helmet. Subspace is actually rotting, he has something known as Crystalrot which is essentially a contagious cancer. Subspace's mask is to prevent him from spreading the rot, and his missing eye is from the rot. Subspace also betrayed Medkit, which is why Medkit is wanted by Blackrock.

Boombox

Boombox is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Boombox is from Playground and he is part of the Playground trio with Coil and Skateboard. Boombox

Vinestaff

Vinestaff is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Vinestaff is from Theives Den, and she works at Slingshot's cafe known as Theives Rest. Vinestaff was part of the Darkage Clan, but she struggled due to her arm not being flesh. Vinestaff's arm is made of wood, and it is seen as a curse by many. If Vinestaff's wooden arm is harmed, it simply grows back. Vinestaff is also Shuriken's sibling.

Medkit

Medkit is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Medkit is originally from Blackrock, he did research with Subspace before he was betrayed, causing him to lose his right eye. Medkit fled to Crossroads when the incident first happened, later joining the Church Of The True Eye for protection from Blackrock and the Banlands. Medkit is a friend of Sword and sees Sword as a younger sibling.
