Meet The Phighters!!

Melee Phighters

Sword

Sword is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Sword is a natural spawn of Lost Temple, and Venomshank took him in as soon as Sword spawned. Now, Venomshank serves as Sword's mentor and guardian. However, Venomshank has given Sword a very sheltered upbringing, leading to Sword being unaware of major events like The Scorch. Sword is also related to the Sword Deities by blood, but he is unaware. Sword does not know that he's related to Banhammer and Flipside (Dom and Valk). Sword's steel gladiatorial helmet is also a gift from Venomshank. Sword is Rocket's best friend, with Medkit being like an older sibling to him and being acquainted with Zuka since he is Rocket's adoptive father.

Banhammer

Banhammer is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Banhammer is a demideity, he is a child of Windforce. Banhammer is an unnatural spawn, and Banhammer is the second demi-deity to ever spawn. Banhammer followed after his cousin, the child of Firebrand, who was his closest friend. Six years before Phighting, Banhammer was appointed as the Warden of Banlands, a facility meant to correct Inphernals and it was made by Firebrand. When Firebrand disappeared, Windforce took over but then left it to Banhammer who took it with pride. Banhammer oversees the main island and smaller police forces stationed in Crossroads. Many hate Banhammer for being a tyrant and being related to the Sword Deities. Banhammer has signifcantly more strength than the average Inphernal, he has an indefinite lifespan and he willingly wears a blindfold to challenge himself against other Inphernals. Banhammer has two sets of eyes like Windforce and wings like Windforce's own that he can summon and desummon as he pleases. Banhammer is not aligned with any factions, prefering to keep to his own. Banhammer and Scythe have a longstanding rivalry that's mainly one-sided.

Ranged Phighters

Shuriken

Shuriken is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Shuriken is an unnatural spawn, spawning right after Vinestaff. Shuriken was accepted immediately by the Darkage Clan, but he was separated from Vinestaff due to her "curse" she spawned with. Shuriken was trained to be a warrior by the Darkage Clan, becoming incredibly skilled and being regarded as a great warrior in the Clan. Shuriken met Slingshot in the Darkage Clan, and later knew Slingshot as a sibling. With Slingshot, Shuriken would go out at night to see Vinestaff, despite it being forbidden. Eventually, it was finally decided for Shuriken to leave Thieves' Den to live in Crossroads with Vinestaff and Slingshot since it was the safest place for Shuriken's siblings. Shuriken didn't take well to the slowness of city life, he felt he didn't have a purpose. To deal with this, Shuriken created a vigilante persona, known as the "Silver Shadow," using it under the cover of night to fight crime in order to keep the streets of Crossroads safe. However, Shuriken's siblings are unaware of his vigilante persona. They are none the wiser as Shuriken shows up for his daytime job at Thieves' Rest, working as a waiter. Shuriken struggles with exhaustion due to staying up late as a vigilante, often falling asleep in the cafe. Despite how much Shuriken cares for his siblings, he keeps to himself and prefers to avoid worrying them about his life. Shuriken also wishes to be reunited with the Darkage Clan.

Scythe

Scythe is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Scythe used to be one of the Redcliff Wranglers and Grappling Hook's significant other. However, when the Scorch happened, Scythe left the wranglers to join what is now The Church Of The True Eye. Scythe also lost her right arm in the event. Scythe is very loyal to the Church, she mostly works as a mob enforcer and she is commanded by Father Overseer. Scythe blindly follows the word of Father Overseer, but she still has complicated feelings about it. Despite this, Scythe is still highly respected and loved by her fellow Church members, she is seen as an older sibling figure the other members can bring their problems to. Having Medkit in the Church now, Scythe got her prosthetic arm from him and her gear has been modified to have a rifle mode instead of only being a green laser scythe, the gear she spawned with. As for Scythe's specialized scope on her eye, she wears it due to her missing eye. Scythe is missing her left eye due to the practices of the Church, and it is replaced by a glass eye. Since she has a missing eye, she has poor depth perception like Subspace. A fun fact, Scythe originally had black horns but they're painted teal to show her devotion.

Scythe also has a deadly reputation with the Banlands police and consistently evades capture. Scythe is very close with Broker, as well as an acquaintance with Medkit. Medkit and Broker both work under Scythe. Before Katana was forced out of the Church, Scythe was a friend.

Hyperlaser

Hyperlaser is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Hyperlaser was spawned in Blackrock, and the Korblox Administration raised him to be a soldier in the military division. Hyperlaser works as a mercenary, taking on a variety of jobs such as being a body guard or assassinations. At some point in Hyperlaser's life, he got involved in an incident that nearly took his life. It left him with burn scars across his body, his horns left as stubs and he was legally blind. Subspace, Hyperlaser's contractor, designed Hyperlaser's signature helmet which allows him to see despite being legally blind. Hyperlaser is one of the few remaining soldiers that's alive, now living in District 4 of Blackrock with a pet cat known as Princess. Princess was found abandoned during one of Hyperlaser's missions in Crossroads. Hyperlaser also struggles to get close to other Inphernals, but recently has become familiar with Katana.

Slingshot

Slingshot is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Slingshot is a natural spawn of Thieves' Den, and he was raised in the Darkage Clan with Shuriken. Shuriken and Slingshot were both raised as warriors, and recently Slingshot left the Darkage Clan to move to Crossroads for a better life. Slingshot does baking and he runs his own cafe known as Thieves' Rest that can be found in the second layer of Crossroads. Slingshot wants to eventually reach the top layer someday. Slingshot lives with Shuriken and Vinestaff in one apartment, people who he considers his siblings. Shuriken and Vinestaff work for Slingshot's cafe. Ever since Slingshot left the Darkage Clan, he considers himself factionless. Slingshot is friends with Skateboard, a neighbor to Katana, and much to Slingshot's annoyance, he regularly interacts with Coil; a patron of Thieves' Rest.

Rocket

Rocket is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Rocket is a natural spawn from Playground, he lived there for the majority of his life as a newspawn. Rocket was forced to leave after an incident that left him missing an arm and a leg. While leaving Playground, Rocket met Zuka and they became very close. Zuka became Rocket's guardian figure, and Zuka took Rocket to Crossroads which is where Rocket has lived ever since. Rocket later met Sword and became best friends with him, and Sword introduced him to Medkit. Medkit ended up making Rocket's prosthetic arm and leg. Now, Rocket works with Zuka in their shop at the top layer of Crossroads, providing artificial gears for other Inphernals and providing general handyman services. Rocket also works with Broker, as much as he doesn't like it. Broker provides the materials used to create artificial gears and other weapons.

Support Phighters

Coil

Coil is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Coil spawned in Playground, part of the Playground trio with Boombox and Skateboard. Coil is a natural spawn from Playground, he was soon picked up by a strange Inphernal who is only known as Wolf. Wolf rescued Coil from the brink of death, raising him as her own. Wolf also mentored Coil, teaching him what he knows now. Coil originally spawned only as Gravity Coil, but with some experimentation he added 3 more modes; those being Speed, Regeneration, and a very dangerous fusion mode. Coil is also a well-known thief within Craterdust Capital, having stolen different technology and crystals from the Korblox Administration. Most often, Coil gives out some crystals to other residents and takes the rest to experiment with at Wolf's abode. Due to these crystals, mainly the one lodged in Coil's right horn, Coil's abilities and strength have a significant boost. With the crystals, Coil can, on rare occations, enter fusion mode for extremely short periods of time. With fusion mode, Coil can cause extreme damage but at the cost of it using much of his energy. While in fusion mode, Coil's right side of his face is covered in a layer of ice-like crystal, which melts once he's out of fusion mode and it leaves his right eye leaking water. After using fusion mode, Coil is knocked unconscious. Coil is friends with Skateboard and Boombox, but Coil refuses to let anyone know his past and doesn't let anyone close to him. Coil is also regularly at Thieves' Rest, where he loves annoying the owner, Slingshot.

Subspace

Subspace is one of the currently 15 Phighters available to play in game.

Subspace is a natural spawn from Blackrock, he was raised by various members of the Korblox Administration, growing into an accomplished scientist in robotics engineering and organic chemistry. Subspace formerly worked with Medkit, developing the first instances of energy crystals together. One issue, however. Subspace and Medkit never got along, so after a violent altercation, Subspace was left with something simply known as "The Rot." Despite being left with the Rot, Subspace continued to do his own research and develop his own kind of energy crystals. Subspace would then go and invent the Biografts, which are now used for many jobs in the Korblox Administration, such as maintenance and infantry. Subspace is well-recognized for his work, so he is very high ranking in the Korblox Administration. Subspace does greatly struggle with the rot, which shows on his body as bright pink lesions across his whole body, rotting away his flesh with no clear cause to it. This mainly affects Subspace's right arm and left eye, which has left him half-blind. Even though the rot is not exactly contagious, Subspace still does produce a very toxic gas within his body that is highly fatal to anyone who inhales it. Since the gas is so toxic, Subspace is required to wear a mask with other Inphernals to prevent poisonings. The Rot does severely affect Subspace's day to day life, and since he is half-blind, he struggles with depth perception in his right eye. Subspace's right arm has limited mobility as well, and he is easily fatigued. The flesh around Subspace's mouth has also begun to rot, leaving him to struggle with eating and being unable to taste anything. Due to these difficulties, Subspace created the Gamma Biograft, a one of a kind Biograft that is essentially a medical assistant for Subspace. Subspace can wear the Gamma Biograft as a suit, letting him do things he normally can't. Since Subspace has been considered unsightly by the higher-ups, he is required to wear the Gamma Biograft suit in public. Subspace does not have many relations besides his past with Medkit and contracting Hyperlaser, a colleague, for work.
